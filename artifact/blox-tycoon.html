HOW TO PLAY
  • Stand on an empty plot's gold CLAIM pad to make it yours.
  • Three buy pads are available at a time. Green means you can afford it, red shows what you still need.
  • Droppers drop cash cubes on the conveyor. Upgraders multiply every cube that passes through.
  • Cubes end in the Collector. Stand on the blue pad to bank them, or buy the Auto Collector.
  • The Second Floor adds a sky conveyor whose cubes drop down through all your upgraders.
  • Build everything to unlock REBIRTH: start over with +50% income forever, stacking each time.
  • Your tycoon saves in this browser. Share the page and a server code to play with friends.
