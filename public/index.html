    HOW TO PLAY
    • Claim a tycoon by standing on an empty plot's glowing CLAIM pad.
    • Buy items by stepping on pads once you can afford them. Green = can buy, red = too expensive.
    • Droppers drop cubes onto the conveyor. Upgraders multiply cubes that pass through them.
    • Cubes end up in the Collector. Stand on the COLLECT pad to move the cash to your wallet.
    • Buy everything to unlock REBIRTH: your tycoon resets but all income gets +50% forever.
    • Your tycoon is kept for 2 minutes if you disconnect. Share the server code to play with friends.
